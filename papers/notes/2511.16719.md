# SAM 3: Segment Anything with Concepts
arXiv 2511.16719v2 (v1 Nov 2025, v2 28 Mar 2026) · Meta Superintelligence Labs
Code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3 · Demo: https://segment-anything.com

## Core contribution
New task **Promptable Concept Segmentation (PCS)**: given a *short noun phrase* ("yellow
school bus"), and/or **image exemplars** (positive/negative boxes), detect + segment + track
**every** matching instance, with persistent identities. SAM 1/2 segmented one object per
prompt; SAM 3 finds *all* instances of a concept. One model = open-vocab detector + tracker.

## Architecture (what matters for using it)
- **850M params**: 450M vision encoder + 300M text encoder (both Perception Encoder, ref
  2504.13181 — the paper already on our second-tier list) + 100M detector & tracker heads.
- **Detector**: DETR-style. Image + text encoded by PE → fusion encoder cross-attends to
  prompt tokens → DETR decoder with learned object queries → box + mask heads.
- **Presence token — the key architectural trick.** Instead of making each object query answer
  "is this a *yellow school bus* and where is it", a single global learned token predicts
  `p(concept present in frame)`, and each query only predicts
  `p(query matches | concept is present)`. Final score = product. Decoupling recognition from
  localization is what makes hard negatives trainable, and it's the biggest ablation win.
  **Practical read-through: SAM 3 gives you a per-frame "is this concept here at all"
  probability that is separate from box quality. That is exactly the signal a retrieval
  scorer wants.**
- **Tracker**: SAM 2 style memory bank, shares the frozen PE backbone. Per frame: detector
  proposes `O_t`, tracker propagates masklets `M_{t-1}` → IoU matching → spawn new masklets
  for unmatched detections. Two disambiguation tricks: (i) suppress masklets whose
  "detection score" (fraction of recent frames matched to a detection) falls below threshold,
  (ii) periodically **re-prompt the tracker with high-confidence detections** to stop drift.

## Headline numbers (conditions attached)
- **LVIS zero-shot mask AP 48.8 vs 38.5 prior best.** SOTA on COCO / COCO-O boxes too.
- SA-Co/Gold (open-vocab, 3 annotators/pair): **>2× the cgF1 of the best baseline (OWLv2)**,
  reaching **74% of estimated human performance**.
- Video PCS (SA-Co/VEval, text prompt): SAM 3 reaches **>80% of human pHOTA**. Baselines
  collapse — GLEE scores 0.0–1.6 cgF1 zero-shot where SAM 3 scores 30–51.
- Ablation of components: swapping in LLMDet as detector (keeping SAM 3 tracker) drops SA-V
  cgF1 from 30.3 → 2.3; swapping the tracker for tracking-by-detection drops to 25.7.
  **The detector is doing most of the work; the tracker is worth ~5 cgF1.**
- Metric note worth stealing: they **only score predictions with confidence > 0.5**,
  deliberately, "to mimic downstream usage and enforce good calibration" — because AP hides
  calibration failures. Main metric `cgF1 = 100 × pmF1 × IL_MCC` (localization × image-level
  presence classification).
- **SAM 3 Agent**: an MLLM proposes noun phrases, prompts SAM 3, inspects returned masks,
  iterates. Zero-shot, this beats prior work on ReasonSeg / OmniLabel / RefCOCO+ / RefCOCOg
  **without any referring-expression training**. Works with several different MLLMs on the
  same system prompt.

## ⚠️ Speed — read this before planning around SAM 3
- **30 ms per image on an H200** with 100+ objects. That is a *single frame on a $30k GPU*.
- **Video latency scales linearly with the number of tracked objects.** To hit 30 FPS they
  parallelise across GPUs: **10 objects on 2×H200, 28 objects on 4×H200, 64 objects on 8×H200.**
- We have a Kaggle P100 or 2×T4. Expect **well over an order of magnitude slower** than the
  quoted figures. A busy CCTV frame (a car park, a crowd) has far more than 10 objects.

## Stated limitations
- **Fails on fine-grained out-of-domain concepts zero-shot** — they name *aircraft types* and
  *thermal imagery* explicitly. Both are plausible in drone footage.
- PCS is defined for **images or short video (≈30 s)**. Not a long-video tool by itself.
- **Simple noun phrases only.** No referring expressions, no multi-attribute queries beyond
  one or two attributes, no reasoning. ("the van that reversed out of bay 3" is out of scope.)
- Cost scales linearly in object count; no shared cross-object memory.
- Hard mode-switch between concept mode and instance mode.
- Fine-tuning on small amounts of in-domain data recovers a lot (their Tab. 2) — not an option
  for us in 7 hours.

## What we steal for a 7-hour hackathon on CCTV/drone footage

1. **Use it as the open-vocab detector in `enrich.py`, prompted by the noun phrases that
   Query-Decouple (Video-RAG, §2411.13093) extracts.** That composition — small VLM emits
   noun phrases → SAM 3 finds all instances → verbalize into text — is the enrich stage.
2. **The presence score is a free retrieval signal.** Per chunk, per concept, store
   `max_frame p(concept present)`. That is a calibrated, cheap, *per-concept* index we can
   retrieve on directly, alongside SigLIP2 embeddings. It's a better version of what T\*'s
   grid scorer computes, and SAM 3 hands it to us for nothing.
3. **Copy the >0.5-confidence evaluation rule and say why.** "We only score detections above
   0.5 confidence, because a security operator acts on thresholded output, not on an AP curve"
   is a credible, product-shaped justification and it costs one line of code.
4. **Copy the tracker's re-prompting trick if we do any tracking**: periodically overwrite the
   tracker's prediction with a high-confidence detection to stop drift. Cheap, big stability win.
5. **SAM 3 Agent's loop is our fallback for behaviour queries.** Our thesis is that noun-phrase
   detection can't answer "did anyone loiter near the gate". SAM 3 Agent shows the accepted
   workaround: let the VLM decompose the hard query into noun phrases, then reason over the
   returned masks/tracks. Our contribution should be to do that decomposition *at index time*
   over motion features, rather than per-query at inference — that's the differentiator.

## Decision for tomorrow (my recommendation)
**Do not put SAM 3 on the critical path.** The speed numbers are for H200s, the download is
large, and object-count-linear video cost is exactly wrong for busy CCTV. Plan:
- **Default**: YOLO-World or a plain YOLO for the enrich stage — fast, predictable on T4.
- **SAM 3 as an upgrade** applied only to the ~10 chunks that survive retrieval (same
  two-stage logic as VideoRAG's re-captioning: cheap at index time, expensive at answer time).
  In that slot its cost is bounded and its quality is worth it.
- **If it works at all, the benchmark is free content**: "we A/B'd YOLO-World vs SAM 3 on the
  enrich stage, here's recall vs index-time cost" is a real measured result either way — and
  if SAM 3 is too slow on our hardware, *that is a finding worth reporting*, not a failure.
  Ties into our "keep a failure case" agreement.
- Also check at 11:00 whether the footage is **thermal or high-altitude aerial** — the paper
  names both as weak spots, and that would settle the choice immediately.
